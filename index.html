Free Sensitivity Finder

Choose your device and aim style. These are community-style starting suggestions—not measured or official settings. The values use a 0–200 scale; if your in-game slider has a different maximum, scale them proportionally.

How to test: Apply the values manually in Free Fire settings. In Training Ground, try 10–15 controlled upward drags at a close target. If aim passes over the head, lower General/Red Dot by 3–5; if it stops below, raise by 3–5. Change one setting at a time.

How to use your settings

01 — Choose

Select your phone and aim goal in the free finder.

02 — Copy

Copy the suggestions and enter them manually in-game.

03 — Practice

Test in Training Ground and adjust by small amounts.

04 — Repeat

Fair-play note: This tool does not connect to Free Fire, inspect your game, or change settings automatically. It provides editable suggestions only. Sensitivity varies by game version, device, screen, FPS, and personal control. Never share your game password, M-Pesa PIN, or OTP.
